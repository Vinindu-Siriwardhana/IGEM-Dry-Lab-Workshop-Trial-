How thick should the patch be?貼片應該有多厚？

A wound that won't heal keeps sending out a warning gas. Our patch sits on that wound, and the bacteria inside it sense the gas and start making a healing medicine. The gas fades fast as it rises, so only the bacteria near the wound ever smell it. The medicine they make has to travel back down, and some is lost on the way.一個無法癒合的傷口會不斷釋放一種警告氣體。我們的貼片就貼在這個傷口上，貼片內的細菌感應到這種氣體後，便會開始製造療傷藥物。氣體向上升時會迅速消散，所以只有靠近傷口的細菌才「嗅」得到它。細菌製造的藥物要向下走回傷口，途中有一部分會流失。

The problem問題

A wound that won't heal一個無法癒合的傷口

It stays red and swollen for weeks. It keeps sending out a warning gas that says "something is wrong here".傷口一連幾個星期都又紅又腫，還不斷釋放一種警告氣體，好像在說：「這裡出事了！」

ScScientists call these chronic wounds and the gas nitric oxide (NO)科學家稱這類傷口為慢性傷口（chronic wound），這種氣體是一氧化氮（NO）
The patch貼片

The gas rises into the patch氣體上升，進入貼片

Our patch is a soft gel stacked in layers. Every layer is full of tiny engineered bacteria.我們的貼片由一層層軟凝膠疊成，每一層都充滿經過改造的微小細菌。

The switch開關

The bacteria sense it and switch on細菌感應到氣體，隨即啟動

When the warning gas reaches them, the bacteria start making a healing medicine.警告氣體一到，細菌便開始製造療傷藥物。

ScScientists call the medicine a therapeutic peptide (TAP2)科學家稱這種藥物為治療性多肽 TAP2（therapeutic peptide）
The journey旅程

The medicine travels back down藥物向下走回傷口

It has to pass through the gel to reach the wound. Some breaks down on the way. Watch the dots fade.藥物要穿過凝膠才能到達傷口，途中有一部分會分解。留意那些逐漸消失的圓點。

Your mission你的任務

More layers, more bacteria. But a longer journey.層數越多，細菌越多，但路程也越長。

A thicker patch holds more bacteria — but the warning gas fades before it reaches the ones near the top, so those never switch on, and the medicine made high up has further to travel. How thick should we make it so the most medicine reaches the wound? You'll build a model to find out.較厚的貼片可以容納更多細菌——但警告氣體還未到達頂部的細菌就已經消散，所以那些細菌永遠不會啟動；而在高處製造的藥物，要走的路也更長。貼片要做多厚，才能讓最多藥物到達傷口？你將會建立一個模型來找出答案。

You just discovered the rule你剛剛發現了規律
change=made−lost
變化=製造−流失

More made than lost: it rises. Equal: it stays flat. More lost: it falls.製造多於流失：上升。兩者相等：保持不變。流失多於製造：下降。
